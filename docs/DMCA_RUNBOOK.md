# DMCA / copyright notice runbook (internal)

**Not legal advice.** Follow counsel-approved procedures. Align all public-facing steps with [app/dmca.tsx](../app/dmca.tsx) and Terms.

## Intake

- **Channel:** Dedicated inbox monitored by operations (use address published in DMCA policy, e.g. info@rawstock.live unless counsel designates dmca@).
- **Log:** Ticket ID, date received, complainant contact, URLs alleged, works identified, jurisdiction (if stated).

## Triage (same business day target)

1. Confirm notice contains required elements per policy (identification of work, identification of material, good-faith statement, accuracy statement, signature).
2. If incomplete: send template “deficient notice” reply listing missing items; do not remove content solely on deficient notices without counsel input.

## Action on complete notice

1. **Preserve:** Snapshot metadata (user, upload time, URL) per retention policy.
2. **Disable access** to allegedly infringing material when policy and counsel guidance require (expeditious under US DMCA safe harbor assumptions).
3. **Notify uploader** (if contact available) with neutral wording and counter-notice path if applicable.
4. **Document** decision and timestamps in the ticket.

## Counter-notice

- Verify counter-notice formal requirements.
- If valid and counsel approves: restore after statutory wait period unless complainant files court action (US framework).

## Repeat infringers

- Maintain internal definition consistent with Terms (e.g. strikes / time window).
- Escalate termination decisions to counsel where unclear.

## Retention

- Retain notices and responses for the period counsel specifies (often multi-year for disputes).

## Escalation

- Ambiguous fair use, non-US claims, or threats of litigation → **stop** and escalate to counsel before public statements.

## Annual review

- Reconcile this runbook with `app/dmca.tsx` and [LEGAL.md](./LEGAL.md); update subprocessors in Privacy Policy if tooling changes.
